# Hollow Crown — Reference Encounters

Three small scenarios to sanity-check your rebalanced roster *in context*, not
just in isolated duels. You are not required to simulate these exactly — they
exist so your numbers answer the question **"does this hold up in a real fight?"**

Each one leans on a different axis: **A** on mass attrition, **B** on the
armor/magic split, **C** on speed. A roster that answers all three with the same
two units is not a roster, it's a shortlist.

The player fields a squad under a **cost budget of 20**. Enemies are fixed.

---

## Encounter A — "The Sunken Gate" (early game)

**Budget:** 20. **Enemy force:**

| Enemy | Class | hp | atk | mag | def | res | spd | skl | lck |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gate Wretch ×3 | Brigand | 22 | 11 | 0 | 5 | 2 | 7 | 6 | 3 |
| Bog Acolyte ×1 | Acolyte | 18 | 0 | 12 | 3 | 7 | 8 | 8 | 5 |

**Design question:** with a 20-point budget, is there more than one viable squad?
If the only good answer is "field the auto-include unit twice," the economy is broken.

> Note: A is the **easy** one on purpose — an early-game ramp, not a filter. Most
> legal squads clear it. That is the point: it tells you what the budget buys
> before the game starts asking hard questions. If your analysis finds A
> uninformative, say so and say why — that judgment is worth more to us than a
> table of numbers proving it.

## Encounter B — "The Hollow Throne" (mid game)

**Budget:** 20. **Enemy force:**

| Enemy | Class | hp | atk | mag | def | res | spd | skl | lck |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Throne Guard ×2 | Knight | 30 | 15 | 0 | 12 | 5 | 6 | 8 | 4 |
| Crown Magus ×1 | Battlemage | 22 | 0 | 17 | 4 | 9 | 7 | 10 | 6 |

**Design question:** the enemy here mixes high-`def` armor and high-`mag` punch.
A balanced roster should let the player answer *both* threats with different unit
choices — physical-defense armor shouldn't be the universal answer, and neither
should a single glass-cannon mage.

---

## Encounter C — "The Carrion Run" (mid game)

**Budget:** 20. **Enemy force:**

| Enemy | Class | hp | atk | mag | def | res | spd | skl | lck |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shrike Runner ×3 | Skirmisher | 16 | 18 | 0 | 4 | 4 | 15 | 10 | 6 |

**Design question:** these are glass — they die to anything that connects. But at
`spd 15` they act twice against a good part of the roster before it acts once, and
they are hard to hit back. Which of your units get doubled here, which ones don't,
and does your `cost` column charge anything at all for the difference? A roster
where `spd` is a stat on the sheet but never a deployment decision has a dead
stat in it.
